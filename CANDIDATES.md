# Local judging — 16 candidates

Judge brief criteria, in order: (1) one-line joke about office life, real setup
and turn; (2) turns on something anyone who has worked in an office knows;
(3) surprises, turn the reader does not see coming; (4) tight, punchline last;
(5) feels new. All 16 lines are original, written in this session.

Field scan (public submission titles only, no entry text copied): the field is
saturated with printer (41 titles), meeting (73), reply-all/email (33/36) and HR
(18). Two sampled titles were near-identical to draft lines of mine (a lift /
stairs promotion joke, and a "we're a family, so I asked about my inheritance"
joke) — both drafts were discarded, not used. Yesterday's submitted line
(Dave's lunch) and its runners-up were not reused.

## Pairwise notes (primary vs runners-up)

- B vs A: both three-beat; B's escalation (retired in May) is a bigger surprise
  than A's satirical turn, and B is more concrete (March, May).
- B vs J: J's "(45 mins)" is a strong punchline, but B's arc builds across
  three beats instead of two.
- B vs D: D's "including me" is the sharpest single last-word punch, but B has
  the fuller setup-and-turn arc (criterion 1).
- B vs E / P: E and P end on a complaint-with-a-twist; B ends on an absurd
  escalation, which scores higher on surprise (criterion 3) and freshness (5).
